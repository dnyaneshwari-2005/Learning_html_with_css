Maharashtra, India, has a rich history dating back to the 3rd century BC. The region was part of the Mauryan Empire, then the Satavahana dynasty, and later saw rule by various Hindu and Muslim dynasties. The Maratha Empire, founded by Shivaji, dominated from the 17th to the 19th centuries. In 1960, Maharashtra was formed as a linguistic state, separating from the larger Bombay state.
